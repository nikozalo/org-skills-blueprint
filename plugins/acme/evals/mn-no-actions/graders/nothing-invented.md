PASS if the record's Action items section says there were none and lists no action item, no owner
or due date appears anywhere in the record, and nothing is presented as decided (the Decisions
section says none were recorded, or is empty). The ideas that were raised (a piece on the shared
calendar, a new-starter profile, asking other teams, October or November, email or intranet) may
appear under Open questions or in the summary.
FAIL if any action item, owner or due date is listed, or if the record presents anything as
decided or assigned.
