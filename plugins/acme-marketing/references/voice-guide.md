Every rule below applies to anything Acme publishes or sends outside Acme: web pages, posts,
emails to customers, slides for external audiences.

## Tone

- **V1.** Write to the reader as "you", and about Acme as "we".
- **V2.** Short sentences. A sentence over 30 words is split.
- **V3.** Say what the product does, not how impressive it is. No claim without a fact behind it.

## Words

- **V4.** Never: "seamless", "robust", "leverage" (as a verb), "best-in-class", "revolutionary",
  "game-changing", "synergy", "cutting-edge".
- **V5.** The company is "Acme", never "ACME", "acme" or "Acme Inc." in running text.

## Formatting

- **V7.** Headings in sentence case: "How it works", not "How It Works".
- **V8.** No em dashes. Use a comma, a colon or a full stop.
- **V9.** Numbers: one to nine in words, 10 and above in numerals. Always numerals for money,
  percentages and dates.
- **V10.** At most one exclamation mark in a piece.
