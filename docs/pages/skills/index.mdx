# Skills overview

Type `/` and a skill's name to run it, such as `/acme:meeting-notes`. In Cowork you can also just ask: a skill starts on its own when your request matches it.

## Plugins

| Plugin | For | Who adds it |
|---|---|---|
| Acme (`acme`) | Everyone | Everyone |
| Acme Marketing (`acme-marketing`) | Anyone who writes for Marketing | Anyone who needs it |

Add a plugin yourself: search for **Acme** in **Customize → Plugins** in Cowork, or in `/plugin` in Claude Code. More in [Start here](/install).

## All skills

| Plugin | Skill | What it is for | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acme | [`/acme:setup`](/skills/acme/setup) | Gives Claude the shared Acme context and your role. Run it first | AI team |
| Acme | [`/acme:meeting-notes`](/skills/acme/meeting-notes) | Turns a transcript into the standard Acme meeting record | AI team |
| Acme | [`/acme:contribute`](/contribute) | Fixes a skill or proposes a new one. Runs in Claude Code | AI team |
| Acme Marketing | [`/acme-marketing:voice-check`](/skills/acme-marketing/voice-check) | Checks a draft against Acme's voice guide | Marketing lead |

Something wrong or missing? [Contribute](/contribute).
