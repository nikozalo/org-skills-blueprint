# Meeting notes

`/acme:meeting-notes` turns a meeting transcript into the standard Acme meeting record. Everything in it comes from the transcript, and every decision and action item carries a quote.

## Run it

```txt
/acme:meeting-notes weekly-ops-sync.txt
```

Give it a file, a Google Doc link, pasted text, or a meeting in a transcription app you have connected. Use the transcript, not someone's summary: the record quotes what people said. In Cowork, sharing a transcript and asking for the action items starts it too.

The first time, it asks where to save your records, and offers to keep the folder as a **My meeting records** line in your instructions.

## What you get

One Markdown file, `YYYY-MM-DD <title>.md`, in your records folder:

```md
# Weekly ops sync

**2026-09-28**

**Spoke:** <names>
**Invited, did not speak:** <names>
**Audience:** Internal: all attendees are from Acme
**Source transcript:** <file name>

## Summary
## Decisions
## Action items
## Open questions
```

An action item needs a named owner and a real commitment, so **an empty action-item list is a normal result**. Near misses go under Open questions. The **Audience** line says who it recommends sending the record to: everyone invited, only the organiser when someone from outside Acme attended, or nobody for a meeting tagged `[confidential]`. Correct the record directly: it is the record.

## What it never does

- Share, send or post the record.
- Record a decision or action item without a quote, or guess an owner or a due date.
- Add analysis or next steps nobody said.

Owner: the AI team.
