# Voice check

`/acme-marketing:voice-check` checks a draft against Acme's voice guide before it goes out: a web page, a post, a customer email, external slides. It finds what breaks the guide and suggests the smallest fix. You decide what to change.

## Run it

```txt
/acme-marketing:voice-check launch-post.md
```

Give it a file, a Google Doc link or pasted text. In Cowork, asking whether a draft is on-brand starts it too.

## What you get

A result line, **Ready** or the number of findings, then a table: the exact words from your draft, the rule they break (such as V4, words to avoid), and a suggested fix. Anything the guide does not cover, such as a claim worth checking, is listed separately.

The guide lives in the Acme Marketing plugin and the Marketing lead owns it. A rule that seems wrong is theirs to change: [Contribute](/contribute).

## What it never does

- Rewrite your whole draft, unless you ask after the check.
- Publish, post or send anything.
- Apply a rule that is not in the guide.

Owner: the Marketing lead.
